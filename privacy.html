Privacy Policy

Master of Command · Maritime Leadership Academy
Last updated: 28 September 2026

In short: Master of Command does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no ads.

Who this policy is from

Master of Command ("the app") is developed and published by Tango Juliet Dev ("we", "us"). You can reach us at tangojulietdev@gmail.com for any question about this policy or the app.

Information we collect

We collect no personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any identifier, and does not have a server that receives your data.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work, your progress is saved locally on your own device using your browser's standard local storage. This includes items such as:

  • Modules, scenarios, and course days you have completed
  • Your experience points (XP), rank, badges, and streak
  • Any notes you type and settings you choose (theme, text size)

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you clear your browser or app data, or uninstall the app, this information is permanently deleted.

Backups you create

The app lets you export your progress as a backup file and import it again later. These files are created and stored by you, on your own device or wherever you choose to save them. We are not involved in and have no access to these files.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

The app contains no analytics, no tracking technologies, and no advertising. We do not use cookies for tracking, and we do not build any profile of you or your usage.

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not collect any. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store; your download and any payment are handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Network and offline use

The app is designed to work offline. It does not require an internet connection to function, and it does not send your information over the network during use.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for adult maritime professionals and is not directed at children. Because we collect no data from anyone, we collect no data from children.

Payment

If you purchase the app, the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card number, bank details, or other payment information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email tangojulietdev@gmail.com and we will respond directly.
